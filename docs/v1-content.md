## Twist Families (Severity 1–4 = Week 1–4)

- **Weather**: Windy → Heavy Rain / Crosswind → Storm → Thunderstorm (Wind Shift can occur)
- **Infrastructure**: Taxiway works → Runway inspection → Runway closure → Power outage (Lit/ILS off)
- **Traffic Surge**: Extra flights → more Medium → Heavy Day (many Large) → Surprise rush hour

## Starting Airport

v1 has one: 1 Medium Asphalt Runway, a small Terminal with 2 Small + 1 Medium Stands, a small Cargo Hub with 1 Medium + 1 Small Stand, a Small and a Medium Holding Bay, ATC Tower, on 3×2 square Land Plots (see game-design.md for the layout). (Hard and Custom modes are parked in later-ideas.)

## Run rules (v1)

- A Run is lost by missing a Daily Quota once: a share (first guess 60%) of the Day's Demand, counted in Movements (each landing and each takeoff).
- Incidents and Crashes cost no money; the Runway or Taxiway blockage (by Size Class) is the penalty. (Crash Fines and the Lower Crash Fines Boost were removed; owned levels are refunded in Air Miles.)
- Saturday is a Rush Day (~2× a normal weekday); Sunday is the Boss Day.
- Weekday traffic is strong enough that an unbuilt airport should start failing by mid-Week 2.
- Fire & Rescue Station (levels 1–5) shortens Crash and Incident blockages.

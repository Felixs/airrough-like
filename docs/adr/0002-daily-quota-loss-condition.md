# Runs are lost by missing a Daily Quota, not by Crashes

Originally three Crash Strikes in a Week ended a Run. Prototyping showed that one unlucky Crash on a one-Runway airport cascaded into three fuel Crashes within an hour, and that flights the airport can't handle simply not coming left weekdays with no pressure to build. We now lose a Run by missing a Daily Quota once. The Quota is a share of the Day's full Demand, counted in Movements, so under-building is what loses. Crashes cost escalating Crash Fines instead, and when no Runway is usable the Airport is Closed and low-fuel aircraft divert rather than crash.

## Considered Options

- **Keep Strikes and soften cascades** (shorter blockages, auto-divert): still makes luck the main loss cause and doesn't fix weak weekday pressure.
- **Player-triggered "Close the Airport" action**: rejected because it lets the Player skip a Boss Day.

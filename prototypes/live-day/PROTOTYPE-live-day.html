THROWAWAY PROTOTYPE

Airport Auto Battler · Does the Live Day feel right?

Question: when an airport is under-built, does it fail in a way the Player can see coming and understand (planes Holding → Fuel Reserve running low → Crash → Strike), and do upgrades in Plan Mode visibly fix it? Also: do Twists, the Curfew, Emergency Actions and Emergency Alerts behave sensibly?

The map is abstract here (no tiles, no pathfinding): Taxiways are just a travel time. Numbers are first guesses, not balance. Same settings + same seed always replay the same Day.
